Privacy Policy

Novasotre bot is a customer-support chatbot that answers customer questions on WhatsApp for businesses. Last updated: October 3, 2026.

Information we process

When you send a message to the bot on WhatsApp, we receive your WhatsApp phone number and the content of your messages. We use them only to reply to your questions about the business you are contacting.

How we use it

Messages are sent to a third-party AI language-model provider to generate the reply. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Storage and retention

Recent messages are kept temporarily in the server's memory so the bot can follow the conversation. This conversation memory is cleared whenever the service restarts. We do not keep a permanent database of conversations.

Human handoff

If you ask to speak with a person, your phone number and last message may be forwarded to the business's staff so they can contact you.

Data deletion
